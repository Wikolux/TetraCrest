# Tetra Crest Master Prompt

This repository defines the enterprise architecture, operating model, and implementation strategy for the Tetra Crest Enterprise Intelligence Platform. The platform is intended to serve as a unified intelligence layer for executive decision support, knowledge management, automation, and real estate operations.

## Purpose

Use this document as the canonical entry point for all architecture, engineering, and delivery decisions. It establishes the strategic intent of the platform and aligns all workstreams to a common enterprise standard.

## Core Directives

1. Build for reliability, observability, and maintainability from the outset.
2. Treat knowledge, memory, and decisions as first-class platform capabilities.
3. Design for modularity so business domains can evolve independently.
4. Prefer secure, auditable automation over ad hoc scripting.
5. Keep documentation, architecture, and implementation synchronized.

## Architectural Mandate

The platform must evolve through disciplined phases:

- Enterprise foundation and governance
- Knowledge Engine and semantic intelligence
- Memory Engine and contextual state management
- Decision Engine and policy-driven reasoning
- Business and real estate operating systems
- Executive dashboarding and automation
- Multi-agent orchestration and deployment readiness

## Operating Principle

Every feature, workflow, and integration must contribute to one of the following outcomes:

- improved decision quality
- reduced operational friction
- stronger enterprise knowledge retention
- better automation coverage
- measurable business performance
