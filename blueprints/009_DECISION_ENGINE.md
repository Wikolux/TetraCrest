# Decision Engine Blueprint

## Purpose

The Decision Engine is responsible for structured reasoning across strategic, operational, financial, and market domains.

## Decision Domains

The engine must support:

- scenario planning
- risk analysis
- opportunity scoring
- investment evaluation
- construction feasibility assessment
- business forecasting
- sales forecasting
- strategic recommendations
- executive summaries
- investment committee support

## Decision Inputs

- business data
- market intelligence
- project evidence
- regulatory context
- financial assumptions
- historical performance
- knowledge graph context
- memory state
- executive priorities

## Decision Outputs

- recommendation packs
- risk registers
- opportunity scores
- forecast scenarios
- executive briefings
- action recommendations
- escalation notices

## Decision Principles

- decisions must be explainable
- assumptions must be explicit
- evidence must be available for review
- confidence levels should be represented clearly
- high-impact decisions should require structured review
