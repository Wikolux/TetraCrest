# Learning Engine Blueprint

## Purpose

The Learning Engine ensures that the enterprise intelligence system improves continuously over time. It evaluates performance, learns from results, detects gaps, and recommends updates to strategy and operations.

## Learning Capabilities

The platform must:

- evaluate responses and recommendations
- compare outcomes against expectations
- track KPIs and performance trends
- learn from failures and near misses
- recommend improvements to operating procedures
- detect skill gaps and training needs
- generate updated playbooks and standard practices
- refine risk models and forecasting logic

## Learning Inputs

- decision outcomes
- project performance results
- forecasting accuracy
- execution quality
- client feedback
- compliance events
- sales outcomes
- market response data
- internal operator feedback

## Learning Outputs

- updated operating procedures
- improved prompts and reasoning strategies
- new decision heuristics
- training recommendations
- process redesign recommendations
- strategic improvement opportunities

## Learning Governance

Learning must be evidence-based and auditable. The platform should distinguish between observed outcomes, inferred causes, and recommended changes.
