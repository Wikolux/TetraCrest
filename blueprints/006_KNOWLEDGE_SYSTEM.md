# Knowledge Engine Blueprint

## Purpose

The Knowledge Engine is the organization’s continuous intelligence repository. It must absorb information from internal and external sources and transform it into usable enterprise knowledge.

## Knowledge Sources

The engine must ingest:

- company documents
- project files
- government regulations
- building codes
- construction standards
- engineering standards
- books
- research papers
- web research
- YouTube transcripts
- market reports
- internal decisions
- meeting notes
- sales history
- client conversations
- CRM data
- external market intelligence

## Knowledge Capabilities

The system must support:

- ingestion and classification
- semantic indexing
- document and source linking
- regulatory mapping
- domain-specific retrieval
- knowledge graph construction
- cross-domain reasoning
- evidence-backed recommendations
- continuous learning and refinement

## Knowledge Objectives

The Knowledge Engine must help the enterprise:

- improve decision quality
- capture and preserve institutional knowledge
- reduce repetition and avoidable mistakes
- identify market, operational, and technical patterns
- support executive advisory and strategic planning

## Knowledge Governance

Knowledge must be ranked by:

- relevance
- authority
- recency
- reliability
- domain applicability
- business impact

## Knowledge Outputs

The Knowledge Engine should produce:

- contextual briefings
- standardized operating guidance
- research summaries
- market insight packs
- compliance references
- decision support evidence
- lessons learned updates
