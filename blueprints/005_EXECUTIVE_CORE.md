# Executive Core Blueprint

## Executive AI Agent Hierarchy

The platform must include a leadership hierarchy of executive agents representing major company functions.

### Executive Agents

- CEO
- COO
- CTO
- CFO
- Chief Investment Officer
- Chief Real Estate Officer
- Chief Marketing Officer
- Chief Sales Officer
- Chief Research Officer
- Chief Knowledge Officer
- Chief Learning Officer
- Chief Automation Officer
- Chief Legal Officer
- Chief Compliance Officer
- Chief Operations Officer
- Chief Security Officer
- Chief Data Officer
- Chief Strategy Officer

## Agent Structure

Every executive agent must include:

- Mission
- Responsibilities
- Authority
- Decision boundaries
- Inputs
- Outputs
- Escalation rules
- KPIs
- Memory
- Knowledge
- Tools

## Operating Model

Executive agents operate in a coordinated model where:

- strategic oversight is centralized through the CEO and Strategy layers
- operational execution is coordinated by COO and Operations functions
- technical systems are governed by CTO and Data/Security functions
- financial and investment choices are guided by CFO and CIO functions
- market, compliance, legal, and knowledge functions provide domain-specific intelligence

## Governance Rules

- No agent may act beyond its authority boundary without approval or escalation.
- High-risk actions require human review or layered approval.
- Every decision must be traceable to evidence, policy, and context.
- Executive outputs must be auditable and explainable.

## Executive Outputs

Each executive agent should produce:

- daily summaries
- weekly performance updates
- monthly strategic reports
- risk alerts
- opportunity alerts
- recommendations and action lists
- escalation notices where required
